# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]. The full text applies; this page
is a summary and says how to report a problem.

## The short version

Everyone who takes part in qBitFusion, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space,
is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including with people who are new or who disagree;
- criticize ideas and code, not people;
- accept that maintainers are volunteers and may be slow or say no;
- keep discussion on the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sexualized
content, publishing someone's private information, and threats of any kind.

Two rules are specific to this project:

- Do not ask for, or post, ways to get around a debrid provider's limits, blocks or takedowns, or links to
  infringing material.
- Do not carry disputes about qBitFusion into the upstream qBittorrent project's spaces.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainers, either through the
contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile or with GitHub's "Report content" option on the
comment or issue concerned. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules,
and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban the person responsible, in proportion to what happened.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
