# qBitFusion logo brief

**Product.** qBitFusion is a desktop BitTorrent client (a fork of qBittorrent). Its one idea: it *fuses two
download sources* (the peer-to-peer swarm and a fast HTTPS "debrid" source) into a single, hash-verified download.

**Interface language.** Restrained and tool-like, in the spirit of Linear, Things 3, Raycast and Transmission:
true-neutral greys (near-black #141414 / off-white #F6F6F4), hairline dividers, small radii, and exactly one
accent colour, "ember" orange #FF5B2E, used sparingly.

**What the mark must be.**
- A flat, geometric symbol with a strong, simple silhouette that still reads at 16 x 16 px (taskbar, tray).
- At most two colours: ember orange #FF5B2E plus one neutral (near-black #141414 or off-white #F6F6F4).
- Idea to express: two streams joining into one; fusion; a download arriving. Abstract is better than literal.
- Confident and a little industrial, like a mark stamped on equipment. Crafted, not generated-looking.

**What it must NOT be (these read as generic AI output).**
- No purple, violet, indigo or blue-to-purple gradients. No gradients at all. No glow, gloss, 3D, shadows or bevels.
- No "white glyph on a gradient rounded square". No generic cloud, lightning bolt, rocket, sparkle or plain down-arrow-in-a-tray.
- No letters, wordmark or text. No mascot. No thin hairline strokes that vanish when small.

**Deliverables.** Four clearly different concepts, each a 1024 x 1024 PNG with the mark centred, generous
padding, on a plain flat background: `concept-1.png` ... `concept-4.png`, saved in this folder.
